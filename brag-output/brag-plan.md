# Brag Plan: Clarion AI

## What is this app?
Clarion AI is an intelligent, objective, and calm AI-curated news platform designed for the discerning reader.

## The angle
A premium, sophisticated news service that cuts through the noise. It feels less like a loud startup and more like an established, intelligent concierge curating the world's most important information just for you.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The screen is dark (hsl(228 15% 7%)). Soft, slow-typing typography appears in stark white: "Clarion AI." The tagline fades in gently beneath it: "Intelligent, objective, and calm."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The AI Analysis view drops in, highlighting the "Sentiment: Positive" badge and concise AI summary bullets.
- The "For You" personalized feed slides in smoothly, demonstrating the intelligent curation engine.
- A quick sequential reveal of AI-generated keywords (e.g., Technology, Global Markets, AI).

## Outro / punchline
The screen clears. A single line remains: "AI-curated news for the discerning reader." The Clarion AI logo settles comfortably in the center.

## User flow worth showing
- Entry: User views the "Latest Analysis" tab.
- Action: The AI sentiment and summary are revealed for a trending article.
- Result: The user switches to the "For You" tab for personalized news.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: Pacing is deliberate with longer holds on text. The visual energy is restrained, elegant, and confident. No flashy animations—just smooth fades and clean slides.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: hsl(228 15% 7%)
- Accent: hsl(243 80% 72%)
- Text: hsl(220 14% 92%)
- Display font: Inter
- Body font: Hanken Grotesk
- Strongest visual element: The clean, dark-mode article cards with AI sentiment badges.

## Share copy (draft)
Cut through the noise. Clarion AI delivers intelligent, objective, and calm AI-curated news directly to you. 

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: Cinematic bed with a low swell, restrained motion-matched accents, and subtle audio-reactive glow.
- Music treatment: Low music bed, gentle fade-in, fading out under the final logo.
- Music cue guidance: 1-2 strong cues for the main reveals; beat-grid not necessary for this deliberate pace.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; background warmth and slight depth on the article cards responding to RMS.
- SFX posture: sparse professional accents; soft ticks for typing text, clean wooshes for card slides.
- Audio-coupled moments: 
  - Scene 1 — typing the hook line with soft key ticks.
  - Scene 2 — smooth card slide matching a subtle whoosh.
- Restraint rule: No chaotic or loud drops. Keep it calm and sophisticated.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
Background is pure dark. Center screen types out: "Clarion AI".
Below it, fades in: "Intelligent, objective, and calm."
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing simulation for "Clarion AI", then crossfade for tagline.
Audio intent: establish a premium, calm atmosphere.
Audio-coupled idea: soft typing ticks for the title.
Music: cinematic bed begins, gentle fade in.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Signal — 6s
The screen crossfades into the "Latest Analysis" view of the app. A dark-mode article card expands to show the AI Summary bullets and a "Sentiment: Positive" badge. 
Sequential/interaction: yes — the sentiment badge and 3 keywords pop in sequentially.
Audio intent: smooth UI interaction.
Audio-coupled idea: soft clicks as the keywords pop in.
Transition mood: smooth slide up → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Concierge — 6s
The view slides to the "For You" personalized feed. 3 article cards slide in one by one.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards sliding in one by one.
Audio intent: clean, professional arrival.
Audio-coupled idea: muted card-slide whoosh.
Transition mood: dramatic wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Outro — 4s
Screen wipes to pure dark.
Text fades in: "AI-curated news for the discerning reader."
Fades out, leaving only "Clarion AI".
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: resolution.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: final chord holds and fades out.
Transition mood: fade to black.
